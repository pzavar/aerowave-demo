What Trip Disruption Protection covers

Payouts are a fixed amount per traveler, based on what happened to your flight.

  • Delay of 120+ minutes

    $150

    Your flight left or arrived two hours or more behind schedule.

  • Cancellation

    $300

    Your flight was cancelled by Aerowave.

  • Delay under 120 minutes

    Not covered

    Shorter delays don't qualify for a payout.

For travelers who added Aerowave Trip Disruption Protection at booking.
